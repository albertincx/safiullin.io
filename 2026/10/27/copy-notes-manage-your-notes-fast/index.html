Copy Notes – Manage Your Notes Fast (Client-Side Local Notepad)

A fast, private, client-side personal notepad with instant 1-click copy to clipboard. All your notes, snippets, prompts, and ideas are stored locally in your browser’s LocalStorage with zero server uploads.

Managing quick notes, code snippets, prompt templates, and frequently used text snippets shouldn’t require complex cloud apps, account logins, or sluggish interfaces.

Copy Notes is an instant client-side notepad built for speed, privacy, and convenience:

  • Instant 1-Click Copy: Copy any note’s text or markdown directly to your clipboard in a single click with instant visual confirmation.
  • 100% Private & Local: Your data lives purely in your browser’s LocalStorage. No servers, no tracking, and no external requests.
  • Fast Search & Filter: Find snippets instantly by keyword or tag (#work, #code, #prompts, etc.).
  • Organize & Pin: Pin key notes to the top and color-code cards for quick visual scanning.
  • Backup & Restore: Export all your notes to a JSON file at any time, or restore from a backup across devices.
  • Works Offline: Accessible anytime, even without an active internet connection.

Enjoy a clean, distraction-free notepad designed to keep your most important snippets always at your fingertips!
