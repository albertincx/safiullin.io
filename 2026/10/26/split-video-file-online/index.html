Split Video Online Instantly without Server

Split, trim, and cut your video files instantly right inside your browser — completely client-side with no backend or server uploads required.

Powered by WebAssembly and FFmpeg, this tool processes files locally on your device in mere seconds. It supports all popular video formats (MP4, WebM, MOV, MKV, and more) and lets you trim single clips, divide long videos into 15s/30s/60s chunks for WhatsApp, Instagram Stories, TikTok, or YouTube Shorts, or split into equal parts. With zero-transcoding copy mode, your video stream is sliced losslessly at lightning speed without any degradation in quality. Safe, private, and works smoothly on both mobile phones and desktop computers!
