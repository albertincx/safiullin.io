Free Online Video Editor (Trim, Crop, Rotate, Speed, Audio, Subtitles)

A free, private online video editor that works 100% in your browser. Trim videos, crop aspect ratios (16:9, 9:16 Shorts/Reels, 1:1), rotate, flip, change speed, apply color filters, add subtitles, and mix background music or voice-over — without server uploads or watermarks.

Why Use an In-Browser Video Editor?

Most web-based video editing services upload your large video files to remote cloud servers, enforce low resolution limits, or stamp distracting watermarks unless you purchase a subscription.

This Online Video Editor runs entirely on your device using client-side HTML5 Canvas, Web Audio API, and MediaRecorder:

  • 100% Confidential & Private: Your videos never leave your computer or phone.
  • Aspect Ratio Formatting: One-click framing for YouTube (16:9), Instagram/TikTok/Shorts (9:16), or Square (1:1).
  • Rotate & Mirror: Rotate 90°/180°/270° or mirror selfie videos horizontally/vertically.
  • Speed Control: Smooth slow-motion (0.25x - 0.75x) or fast-forward (1.25x - 4.0x) with real-time audio sync.
  • Color Grading & Filters: Instant B&W, Warm Sepia, Vintage Cinema, Vibrant, Cyberpunk, and brightness/contrast sliders.
  • Text & Watermark: Add custom titles, handles, or captions with positioning and styling.
  • Multi-Track Audio Mixing: Adjust original video audio, add your own background music (MP3/WAV), or record live voice-overs with your microphone.
  • Instant Frame Snapshot: Download any frame in high quality with all edits and filters applied.

Related In-Browser Video Tools

  • 🔗 Merge Video Files Online: Join two or more video clips into one continuous movie without re-encoding.
  • ✂️ Split Video File Online: Cut large video files into multiple clips or extract specific segments instantly.
